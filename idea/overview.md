## The idea

- **Problem:** conferences aren't fun, so people don't get or share as much out of them. Our goal: "Make Conference Fun."
- **Who has it:** attendees (want connections, opportunities, knowledge), organizers and sponsors (want visibility/exposure, candidates), speakers (want exposure, a following, and to feel good about what is essentially charity work).
- **What we build:** Conquest ("conference quest"). Organizers set up a themed dungeon for their conference. Attendees sign up with LinkedIn, get an editable character, and see a dungeon map where talks and speakers are bosses picked for their goals by Elasticsearch. They gain XP by checking into talks, photos, networking, parties and quests. After the conference, attendees get a customizable LinkedIn post, organizers an after-action report, and speakers the coins and notes attendees gave them.
- **Why it wins (against the judging criteria):** judging criteria not filled in yet. Tracks discussed: Elasticsearch (main), plus a second track still open (Guitar or Sonar).

## The demo in one sentence

A two-minute video: an organizer builds the dungeon, an attendee signs up and sees their map and quests, earns XP at the conference (real life next to our product), then everyone gets their post-conference recap, closing on how Elasticsearch powers it.
