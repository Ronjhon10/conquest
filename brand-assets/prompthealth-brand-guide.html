PromptHealth // Brand guide // v1

A clinic computer
from the future.

Fonts, colors, effects, motion and cursor rules lifted from prompthealth.com/pps/2025. The screen is a dark CRT monitor in a black bezel. Labels talk like a terminal. Headlines are thin and quiet. Color comes from one live theme at a time.

001//TY

Typography

Two faces, never more. VCR OSD Mono for every label, number, button and system message, always uppercase and tracked wide. PP Neue Montreal at weight 100 (Thin) for headlines and body. Base size is 19.2px. Install both fonts; VT323 and Inter Tight are the stand-ins this page falls back to.

002//CO

Color

The screen is near-black #1C1C1A inside a pure black bezel. Text is white. One theme color drives every line, label and fill on a screen. Cyan is the default; each section swaps in its own. Click a swatch to copy it. Use the tabs above to see the theme change everything.

003//FX

CRT effects

Everything sits under a fixed, click-through overlay. Three layers stack on top of the page, plus a chromatic split on display text. This page runs the same overlay.

Scanlines

2px clear, 2px black at 30%. Drifts 10px down over 8s, forever.

RGB mask

Vertical 1px red, green, blue stripes at 10%. Flickers opacity 1 → .98 every 0.1s.

Vignette

Radial: clear to 50%, 20% black at 80%, 60% black at the edge.

Stand by

Chromatic split: magenta +1px, cyan −1px at 30%, plus a 0.5px blur on big type.

005//MO

Motion

Fast and springy. Most things finish in 0.3s with a small overshoot. Panels slide in over 0.5s and settle softly. Marquees and scanlines run linear and never stop.

Snap · 0.3s · cubic-bezier(0.19, 1, 0.251, 1.147) · hovers, cursor, fills
Out · 0.3s · cubic-bezier(0.19, 1, 0.22, 1) · transforms
Soft · 0.4s · cubic-bezier(0.075, 0.82, 0.165, 1) · reveals
Slide · 0.5s · cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) · panels, carousels
Ticker

Header marquee loops venue, dates and year, separated by |. 30s linear (50s for slower bands).

Text scramble

On hover, mono text cycles random glyphs and resolves left to right in about 0.4s. Loading copy types itself: "Accessing files..."

Sound

UI clicks have sound (Howler). The header shows a Sound on ■ toggle. Always offer mute.

006//CU

Cursor and scroll

The system cursor is hidden. A 14.4px white dot with mix-blend-mode: difference follows the mouse, so it inverts whatever is under it. Clicks leave a ripple ring. Over maps and drag areas it turns into a full-screen crosshair.

Dot mode · click anywhere
Crosshair mode · hover, press
Cursor spec
  • Dot: 14.4px white circle, difference blend, follows the pointer 1:1.
  • On links and cards the dot grows; the cursor style is still pointer underneath.
  • Crosshair: 1px lines at white 30% across the whole viewport, a 28.8px square with a 2px white border, solid center. Snaps with the 0.3s overshoot ease.
  • Draggable things (stickers) use grab.
  • Touch devices: no custom cursor at all.
Scroll spec
  • The home screen does not scroll. It is one fixed monitor: black 20px bezel, header bar, centered content.
  • Opening a card swaps the screen to that section and re-tints everything with its theme color.
  • Sections move sideways, not down: arrow buttons and square page dots drive a horizontal track that slides 0.5s.
  • Tabs at the top jump between sections; a ✕ returns home.
  • The mouse wheel does nothing on the main screen. Long reading pages (like this guide) may scroll, with the tab bar sticky.
