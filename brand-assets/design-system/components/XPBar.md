# XPBar

Level, progress to the next level, and the exact count: `LV 4 [bar] 390/500`.

- Track is white with an ink border and `radius-bar`; the fill is blue, its width the share of the level earned.
- Give the track `role="img"` and an `aria-label` like "390 of 500 XP".
- On the map header it shrinks to a 92px track with a 12px height.
