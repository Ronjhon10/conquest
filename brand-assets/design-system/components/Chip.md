# Chip

A small mono tag for facts: times, rooms, XP amounts, step counts and map tags.

- `cq-chip` (ink with white text) for neutral facts, `cq-chip--blue` for XP you can earn, `cq-chip--light` (white with border) for upcoming times on the map.
- Content is a value, not a sentence: `2:00 PM`, `Hall C`, `+40 XP`, `Done`.
