# Card

A white panel with a 2px ink border, `radius-lg` and `shadow-md`; the next-boss card is the main one.

- Content order for a talk: chips (time, room, XP), title in `title` style, speaker as `Name [Speaker]` in `small`, one line on why it matches the attendee's goals, then one `cq-btn`.
- A card may hold dungeon art (a photo, a room strip) edge to edge, divided by a 2px ink rule.
- Over the map, a card floats above the action bar with 16px left and 22px right margin (room for the shadow).
