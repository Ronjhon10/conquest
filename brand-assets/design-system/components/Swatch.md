# Swatch

A square colour choice for the character's accent. Unselected has a 3px ink shadow; selected gets `ring-selected` (a ground gap, then a blue ring).

- Use a `<button>` with `aria-pressed` and an `aria-label` naming the colour ("Blue accent").
- The swatch colours are character options, not brand colours, but keep them to the quiet set: blue, white, steel, near-black.
