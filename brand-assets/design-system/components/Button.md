# Button

The primary action: blue fill, uppercase display label, hard `shadow-md`, presses into its shadow on hover.

- `cq-btn` for in-card actions ("Check in →"), `cq-btn cq-btn--cta` for the one full-width action at the bottom of a screen ("Enter the dungeon →"), `cq-btn cq-btn--secondary` for a white alternative.
- One primary button per card, one call to action per screen.
- Labels are verbs: Check in, Enter the dungeon, Back to map. An arrow `→` may follow.
- Leave 6px on the right for the shadow. Use `<button>` for actions and `<a>` for navigation.
