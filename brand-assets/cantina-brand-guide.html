Aa

Maricle, regular weight 400 (it draws heavy). Headlines, buttons, names, section labels.

Body is 16px on a 24px line. Never set running text in the display face.

Motion

Scroll and cursor

What we saw while scrolling and hovering the reference site, and how to build it.

Section color

The page background fades to the next section's color as that section reaches the middle of the screen. Sections themselves are transparent; the color lives on body.

transition: background-color .6s ease-in-out

Scrolling

Native scrolling. No smooth-scroll library, no scroll hijacking, no snapping. Long logo and photo rows are horizontal carousels moved by Left and Right buttons.

scroll-behavior: auto

Cursor

The normal system cursor everywhere: arrow on the page, pointer on anything clickable. No custom or trailing cursor.

cursor: pointer (controls only)

Hover

Controls press into their offset shadow: move by the shadow's offset and drop the shadow. Text links shift color. Everything uses one quick linear timing.

transition: all .2s linear

Reveal

Section labels appear in a few hard steps with a sideways jitter, like a terminal redraw. Body content is visible at rest and never waits on scroll to appear.

animation: .5s steps(5)

Always there

The gradient register bar stays pinned to the bottom of the screen and the coral back-to-top button floats above it on the right.

position: sticky; bottom: 0
